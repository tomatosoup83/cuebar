Cuebar 0.6.0 — the three most important changes

## Themes

Tahoe keeps Apple's Liquid Glass, exactly as before. **Album Art** tints the whole
panel — background and glass — with the cover you're playing. Pick one in
**Settings → Theme**, or type `theme `.

## Follow the Highlighted Row

An option for the Album Art theme: the panel's colour follows whatever you
highlight instead of the playing track, fading from album to album as you move.
There's a short pause before each change, so cycling through results never strobes.
If a cover has no colour of its own, the panel keeps the playing track's.

## Switch themes by typing

Type `theme ` — with a space — and the search box shows a **Theme** filter listing
your options, so you can change themes (or toggle the follow option) without
leaving the keyboard. Settings itself is now keyboard-navigable too: ↑/↓ to move
between rows, ⏎ to run the highlighted one.
