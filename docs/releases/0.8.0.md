Cuebar 0.8.0 — the three most important changes

## Browse by Artist

Type `artist ` and the field becomes an **Artist** filter: every row is an artist,
matched by name. Press ⏎ on one to open their page — a shuffled sample of their
songs, ready to play. Start typing on the page to filter that artist's catalogue,
and Esc or ⌫ on an empty field takes you back to the search you came from.

Artists also appear in an ordinary search when their name matches, so the page is
reachable without the scope. Compilations stay together under their album artist
(*Various Artists*) rather than splitting into every performer's page.

## Open an Album

Press **⌘⏎** on any library album — or **View Album** in the **⌘K** menu — to see
its own view: the cover, a one-line summary (`2007 · Alternative · 10 tracks ·
42 min`), and the tracks in their **original running order**, numbered as they
appear on the album (`1-3` style numbers on multi-disc sets). Type to filter the
tracks; Esc goes back to where you were, with the same list and the same
highlighted row.

## Run Cuebar From Anywhere

Cuebar now answers the `cuebar://` URL scheme, so Raycast, Shortcuts, a Stream Deck
or a shell script can drive it without opening the palette. The text is exactly
what you'd type into the palette:

```
cuebar://run?command=next
cuebar://run?command=album%20after%20hours
cuebar://rebuild
```

Cuebar shows its usual toast for the result, so the launcher stays silent. The
launcher never needs its own Music permission — Cuebar makes the request. A
ready-made **Raycast extension** is in `raycast/`, with Run Cuebar Command plus
one-key Pause, Play, Next, Previous and Toggle Shuffle.

---

Also in this release: **Album Art v2**, an opt-in theme that builds its colours
from distinct colour clusters rather than one histogram bucket, with a **Global
Colours** option for a flat, gradient-free wash. **View Album** and **Open Artist**
join the ⌘K menu. The `theme ` filter lists both Album Art themes, and the
follow-the-highlighted-row option now works under Album Art v2 as well. The library
index also reads each album's year and genre, so album summaries have something to
show; that information fills in during the first launch after updating.
