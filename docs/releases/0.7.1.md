Cuebar 0.7.1 — the three most important changes

## A New Default Look

Cuebar now opens in **Album Art**: a light gradient drawn from album art washes the
panel behind the glass, and the panel's colour **follows whichever row you
highlight**, fading from album to album as you move.

It's the new out-of-the-box look, so when you update your theme is switched to it
**once**. Prefer plain glass? Choose **Tahoe** in Settings, or type `theme ` — from
then on your choice is kept and never overridden again.

## Home Screen

Open Cuebar and the track you're on sits at the top in a large, glowing card —
artwork, progress, and clickable play/pause, shuffle and repeat, with **Return**
toggling playback. Below it is a **Recently Played** shelf: your last songs, newest
first, each labelled with how long ago you played it.

The shelf is smarter than Music's own history. Music doesn't stamp streamed or
partly-played tracks, so Cuebar notes what it sees playing and merges the two —
every song appears once, at its most recent play, and the track currently on is
left off because the card above it already is that track.

## Quick Actions

Press **⌘K** on any row for a Raycast-style menu: **Play/Run**, **Like** (or
**Remove from Loved**), **Add to Playlist…** with a submenu of your own playlists,
**Open in Music**, and a red **Dismiss**. There's a **"Search for actions…"** field
at the bottom — start typing to filter — and ↑/↓, ⏎ and esc all work there.

Like and Add to Playlist talk to Music directly, so the heart you set here is the
same one you see in the Music app.

---

Also in this release: toasts no longer resize or smear — messages now appear at
their final size and swap in place — a "Searching…" state so a slow catalog lookup
no longer reads as "No matches", and faster recently-played lookups in a single
bulk read. If you're coming straight from 0.6.0, this also includes everything from
0.7.0.
