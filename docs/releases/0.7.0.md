Cuebar 0.7.0 — the three most important changes

## Home Screen

Open Cuebar and the track you're on sits at the top in a large, glowing card —
artwork, progress, and clickable play/pause, shuffle and repeat, with **Return**
toggling playback. Below it is a new **Recently Played** shelf: your last songs,
newest first, each labelled with how long ago you played it (`Just now`, `5m ago`,
`Yesterday`).

The shelf is smarter than Music's own history. Music doesn't stamp streamed or
partly-played tracks, so Cuebar also notes what it sees playing and merges the
two — every song appears once, at its most recent play, and the track currently
on is left off because the card above it already is that track. With a shelf on
screen the transport commands step aside (the card has those controls); anything
time-sensitive, like an available update, stays.

## Quick Actions

Press **⌘K** on any row for a Raycast-style menu of things you can do with it:
**Play/Run**, **Like** (or **Remove from Loved**), **Add to Playlist…** with a
submenu of your own playlists, **Open in Music**, and a red **Dismiss**. There's a
**"Search for actions…"** field at the bottom, so you can start typing to filter;
↑/↓ move, ⏎ runs, and esc backs out or closes.

Like and Add to Playlist talk to Music directly, so the heart you set here is the
same one you see in the Music app.

## Album Art Comes Alive

The **Album Art** theme is now in motion. Behind the glass, the cover's colours
drift as a slow, never-quite-repeating wash; behind the artwork, a soft glow
breathes while music plays. Both freeze where they are the moment you pause, and
both respect **Reduce Motion**.

---

Also in this release: toasts no longer resize or smear — messages now appear at
their final size and swap in place — plus a "Searching…" state so a slow catalog
lookup no longer reads as "No matches", and faster recently-played lookups in a
single bulk read. Experimental branch builds now show a small orange flask in the
footer; release builds are unchanged.
